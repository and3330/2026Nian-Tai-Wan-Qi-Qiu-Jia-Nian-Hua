---
name: Bilingual website scope
description: User-approved language scope and the decision to send bilingual transactional emails.
---

Keep Traditional Chinese and add English switching across public pages, purchase flows and all admin screens. Keep existing banner images unchanged.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected bilingual mode and the scope including the entire admin and buyer notifications, rather than replacing Chinese.

**How to apply:** Preserve original user-entered names, contact information and identifiers. Language changes must not clear forms or carts. Translate editorial copy without altering prices or policies.

Buyer notifications use Chinese and English together, including existing orders.

**Why:** Existing orders have no recorded language preference; bilingual messages make English information available without guessing the recipient's language or changing historical order data.

**How to apply:** Keep Chinese template content intact and maintain equivalent English transactional content. Do not claim a site is WCAG 2.2 compliant based solely on localization or an IAAP-qualified person's involvement.
